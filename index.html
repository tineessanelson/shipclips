Questions

Do I need to send footage?

No. We build the video from your website, your brief and any screenshots you would like included.

Does the video include music?

Videos are delivered without sound. Most people watch X and LinkedIn with the sound off, so every video tells its story with text and motion. On TikTok and Instagram, you can add a sound from the app's own library.

Will you keep my launch private?

Yes. We never share your brief, your product details or your video before your launch, and we never use your video as a sample without your permission.

How are the videos made?

We write and design each video with AI-assisted tools, and a person reviews every video before it is delivered.

What if you miss the 48 hours?

If your first draft arrives more than 48 hours after we receive your payment and brief, we will refund your order in full.

Who owns the video?

You do. You can post it anywhere, as often as you like.
