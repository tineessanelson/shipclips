Step 2 · The script

Five screens, each one readable

We read the website, pull the founder's own wording, and write one idea per screen. Each screen stays up long enough to read at a normal pace, about four words a second, so nobody has to pause the video to catch it.

1 · The hookWho it is for and the promise, in six words.
2 · What you getThree concrete features, ticked off one by one.
3 · How it worksThe whole process in three short steps.
4 · The differenceWhy this instead of the usual way.
5 · The call to actionName, one clear next step, and the web address.
Step 3 · The edit

Built to hold attention on a muted feed

  • Fast cuts. A new screen every two to four seconds, with quick slide-in transitions.
  • Words that land one at a time. Headlines pop in word by word, so the eye follows the sentence.
  • A progress bar. A thin bar across the top shows how much is left, which keeps people watching to the end.
  • No sound needed. Most people scroll X and LinkedIn with the sound off, so every video tells its story in text and motion.
  • Your brand colors. We match the colors on your website, and you can ask for exact ones.
  • Only your claims. We use your own wording and real features. We never add numbers or promises you did not make.
Step 4 · Delivery

Checked, then sent to you

Before anything is sent, a person checks every screen of the finished video for spelling, accuracy and anything cut off. You receive a 1080 × 1920 MP4 by email within 48 hours, and two rounds of changes are included. The video is yours to post anywhere.
