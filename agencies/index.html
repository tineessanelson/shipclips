Questions

Can I deliver the videos under my agency's name?

Yes. Pack videos carry no ShipClips branding, and you can present them as part of your own service.

What do you need for each video?

The product's website, a style choice and a sentence on what's launching. Logos and screenshots are welcome but optional.

Do the videos have sound?

They're silent by default, because most people watch X and LinkedIn with the sound off. Every video tells its story in text and motion.

Will you keep client launches private?

Yes. We never share a brief, a product or a video before its launch, and we never use client videos in our own marketing without permission.

How are the videos made?

We write and design each video with AI-assisted tools, and a person reviews every screen before it's delivered.

What if a video is late?

If a first draft arrives more than 48 hours after its brief, that video is credited back to your pack.
